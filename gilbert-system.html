GILBERT
SYSTEM.

A steering wheel PCB and telemetry system designed as part of a solar car team that I am apart of. I led the development of this system. The system acted as the control systems for the newest car of the team, which will be racing during the summer of 2027 at the Texas Motor Speedway.

Year 2026
Project Solar Car
Overview

ELECTRONICS FOR A SOLAR CAR.

Gilbert utilizes an Arduino Nano (I shouldn't have used it) which is connected to a buck converter, MAX485 module to read data from the MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracker) and display that data. There is also 7 buttons that the driver uses. This includes left turn, right turn, horn, hazard, toggle next screen, toggle back screen, and headlights.

The system was a basic PCB, using mainly through-hole components and devmodules. The next version of Gilbert that I will be developing in the upcoming year will consist of mainly SMD components and ICs. There will be many changes coming to this system to make it more advance and driver-friendly.

ONE PART OF A MUCH LARGER MACHINE.

Working on a system that belongs inside a vehicle means the electronics cannot be designed completely independently.

The system reads and displays data to the driver, displaying data such as the PV (Photovoltaic) Voltage, Battery voltage, Battery State of Charge, Speed, etc.
