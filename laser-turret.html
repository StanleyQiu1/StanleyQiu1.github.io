Overview

AN EARLY STEP INTO ROBOTICS.

This was one of my first projects combining electronics and mechanical movement.

The turret uses a joystick for control and an Arduino Uno to control its movement. Designing the structure also gave me experience with CAD and 3D printing.

3D Printing CAD Arduino Uno
Controller Arduino Uno
Input Joystick
Mechanical 3D Printed
Year 2023
Build

ELECTRONICS MEET MECHANICAL DESIGN.

The project required the mechanical frame, electronic control and software to work together.

The joystick provides user input while the Arduino controls the movement of the turret.
