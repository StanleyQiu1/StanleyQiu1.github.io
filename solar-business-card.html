Overview

MAKING A BUSSINESS CARD MORE THAN A PIECE OF PAPER

I alwayds thought that paper bussiness cards were not that memorable. After watching a few videos on other PCB bussiness cards I decided to make my own.

I used the skills I learned from developing the solar/electrical system on my Solar Car Team to implement the solar circuit on my PCB.

STM32 KiCad PCB Design Solar Embedded
Type Custom PCB
Power Solar
Controller STM32
Design Tool KiCad
Design

FROM SCHEMATIC TO PCB.

I designed the circuit and PCB in KiCad. It is powered by 4 KXOB25-05X3F-TB solar cells. The power is regulated through a TPS63031DSKR Buck Boost IC, providing a constant 3.3V that powers the rest of the electronics.

The rest of the circuit includes an STM32C011F6P7 Microcontroller for the computations, and a 0.96" I2C OLED as the display. There consists of two buttons that allow for the user to play the game that is programmed, which I currently have as the dinosaur game. It was programmed through STM32CubeProgrammer with C. When the User is not playing the game, the screen will display a bar of text that scrolls through my skills and ends with a QR code to this website.
