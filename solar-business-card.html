A custom electronic business card powered by four solar cells. This project combines PCB design, embedded electronics and solar power into a functional business card.

Year 2026
Status Rev A
Overview

MAKING A BUSINESS CARD THAT ACTUALLY DOES SOMETHING.

I wanted to make a business card that was more than just a piece of paper. The goal was to design a custom PCB that could use solar energy to power its electronics.

This project gave me experience with PCB layout, component selection, embedded electronics, power management and designing around limited available energy.

STM32 KiCad PCB Design Solar Embedded
Type Custom PCB

Design

FROM SCHEMATIC TO PCB.

I designed the circuit and PCB in KiCad. One of the important parts of the project was fitting the electronics into the size and shape of a business card.

I also had to think about how the solar cells, power circuitry and microcontroller would work together while keeping the board practical and visually interesting.
